## Addresses

- Address points: U.S. Department of Transportation, National Address Database (a work of the U.S. federal government, public domain, 17 U.S.C. § 105).
- Street names and address ranges for Puerto Rico, Guam, the U.S. Virgin Islands, the Northern Mariana Islands, American Samoa, Hawaii, Michigan, Mississippi, Nevada and New Hampshire: U.S. Census Bureau, TIGER/Line Shapefiles 2025 and the 2020 ZCTA to place relationship file (public domain).
- Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Canada. Source: Statistics Canada, The Open Database of Addresses (ODA), version 1.0, compiled from the open address data of the provincial, regional and municipal governments named in the ODA's `Data_Sources.csv`, each under its own open licence. Providers whose data carries no open licence were left out (City of Fredericton, Cornwall, Columbia-Shuswap, City of West Kelowna, Middlesex County).

## Names and companies

- U.S. Census Bureau, "Frequently Occurring Surnames from the 2010 Census"; U.S. Social Security Administration, "Popular Baby Names" (U.S. Government works).
- Gouvernement du Québec, Retraite Québec, "Banque de prénoms – Filles" and "– Garçons", licence CC BY 4.0.
- Source : Insee, "Fichier des prénoms" (données 1900–2022, publié en 2023) et "Fichier des noms de famille" (naissances 1891–2000, édition 2008), Licence Ouverte / Open Licence v2.0 (Etalab). The data was reduced to counts per name and combined; the meaning of the information is not altered.
- Global Legal Entity Identifier Foundation (GLEIF), Level 1 golden copy and ISO 20275 entity legal forms code list, CC0 1.0.
- Contains public sector information from Companies House (Free Company Data Product, 2026-11-01), licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0 (Creative Commons Attribution).
- U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, EDGAR company names (`cik-lookup-data.txt`), public information.
