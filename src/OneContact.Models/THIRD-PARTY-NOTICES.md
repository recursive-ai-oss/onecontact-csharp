The model weights were trained on the open data below, none of which is packed or redistributed: only the learned weights are. Each source is credited as its publisher asks. Records were reduced to address fields and rendered into free-text layouts of our own, with recipient and organization lines we invented, and in some countries street names, house numbers or postcodes were drawn (see `PROVENANCE.md`). The pinned files and their SHA-256 are in `PROVENANCE.md`.

- Street names and address ranges for Puerto Rico, Guam, the U.S. Virgin Islands, the Northern Mariana Islands, American Samoa, Hawaii, Michigan, Mississippi, Nevada and New Hampshire: U.S. Census Bureau, TIGER/Line Shapefiles 2025 and the 2020 ZCTA to place relationship file (public domain).

Source: Statistics Canada, The Open Database of Addresses (ODA), version 1.0, compiled from the open address data of the provincial, regional and municipal governments named in the ODA's `Data_Sources.csv`, each under its own open licence. Providers whose data carries no open licence were left out (City of Fredericton, Cornwall, Columbia-Shuswap, City of West Kelowna, Middlesex County).

## Contact blocks

The contact-span head trained on text assembled by our own templates from the address, name and company sets credited above, plus word lists of our own. Telephone numbers in the blocks are generated from example numbers in the libphonenumber metadata (Apache-2.0, Copyright Google Inc. and the libphonenumber contributors) with randomized tail digits. No further source.

## Runtime libraries

This package depends on `RecursiveAI.OneNlp.Neural` and `RecursiveAI.OneModel`, which carry their own notices.
